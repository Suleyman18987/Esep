# Есеп Web

Mode: Operate. User-authorized web/PWA counterpart to the existing Есеп 2.4 native Android app, supporting iPhone, Android and desktop. Reproduce the approved Wave, Orbit and Prism designs, their three palettes, actual 3D assets, eight mathematical models, RU/KK copy, formula sheets, grouping and keypad behavior. No database, authentication or account sync. Numeric drafts are memory-only. Vercel hosting from GitHub web/ root is authorized. No unrelated deployment or paid resources.
